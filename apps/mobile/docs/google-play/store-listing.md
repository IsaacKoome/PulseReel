# MimiReel store listing draft

## Main listing

**App name (30-character limit)**  
MimiReel

**Short description (80-character limit)**  
Star in short AI movies created from your clip and one scene idea.

**Full description**

MimiReel turns a short clip and one scene idea into a vertical AI-generated movie starring you.

Record or choose a clip, capture a clear identity frame, describe what happens, and let MimiReel build a short cinematic scene. Keep a result private while you review it, publish it when you are ready, or create another take.

With MimiReel you can:

• Create short AI-generated movie scenes from your own footage  
• Choose a story idea in your own words  
• Review a generated scene before publishing  
• Watch public movies in a vertical feed  
• Like, comment, follow creators, and share movie links  
• Report a movie or comment and block a creator  
• Request deletion of your account and associated data in the app

AI-generated video can make mistakes, change visual details, or invent elements. Review every result before sharing it, and only upload people or material you have permission to use. Do not use MimiReel for impersonation, harassment, non-consensual content, fraud, or other harmful activity.

MimiReel is in beta. Generation availability and output quality may vary while the product improves.
